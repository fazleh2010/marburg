Ada Lovelace

Pioneer of Computing | 1815–1852

Biography

Augusta Ada King, Countess of Lovelace, is widely regarded as the first computer programmer. She worked on Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine and created the first algorithm intended for processing by a machine.

Learn more on Wikipedia or her Wikidata entry (Q7259).

Timeline

1842 Translated and annotated Menabrea’s paper on the Analytical Engine.
